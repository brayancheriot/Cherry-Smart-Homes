Questions landlords ask

Good to know

How much do you charge?

Our fee depends on the service and the number of units. Send us your details and we'll give you a clear quote before you commit.

How do I receive my rent?

Tenants pay through M-Pesa and every payment is recorded. We send your rent to you and share a statement so you always know where the money is.

How do you choose tenants?

We check each applicant's background and references before any lease is signed, and you stay informed throughout.

Can I still keep control of my property?

Yes. You see everything on your owner dashboard and you approve major decisions such as big repairs.

Do you manage only Nairobi properties?

We focus on Nairobi and surrounding areas. If your property is elsewhere, message us and we'll let you know if we can help.
