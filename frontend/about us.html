Our story

Why we started

Finding a good house in Nairobi can mean endless calls, fake listings and unclear prices. For landlords, chasing rent and handling maintenance is just as tiring.

We started Cherry Smart Homes to fix both sides: every home we list is checked before it goes online, prices are shown upfront, and rent is tracked through M-Pesa so landlords and tenants always know where they stand.

Today we help people find homes in Kawangware and across Nairobi, and manage properties for owners who want peace of mind.
