Good to know

Common questions

How do I book?

Choose a stay, enter your dates and tap Book on WhatsApp. We confirm availability and share payment details.

How do I pay?

You can pay by M-Pesa. We'll send you the details on WhatsApp once your dates are confirmed.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Ask us if you need an early arrival or late departure.

Can I cancel or change my dates?

Yes, message us as early as you can. Our cancellation terms are shared when you book.

Is there parking and security?

Most of our stays have secure parking and 24-hour security. Check the tags on each listing.
